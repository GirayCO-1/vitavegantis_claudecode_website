## Clean Label

According to Jump Rope Innovation, a major development in plant-based products will be natural labels built from real plants.

Some observers suggest that as the novelty of plant-based options wears off, consumers will pay ever closer attention to whether these products are genuinely healthier choices — and to whether they cost more.

Another point of view frames it this way: plant-based products will become the new standard, because they have moved past being a novelty people buy on hype and become a genuinely healthy food. The younger consumers most likely to buy plant-based products are a conscious group — they read the ingredients list and want to understand what goes into their bodies.

## Meat and Seafood

According to Datassential's 2023 Food Trends report, "42% of consumers plan to buy plant-based meat products in 2023."

As the plant-based meat industry keeps growing, the report makes clear that the keys will be a persuasive message and products that are competitively priced while tasting as good as conventional meat. Greater variety will fuel the sector's rapid growth in the years ahead.

A great entry point into more specialised plant-based products — whole cuts, for example — could be chef-created dishes in restaurants, where a first taste can be a positive experience that encourages people to bring plant-based meat home too. With renowned chefs across the country and around the world partnering with alternative-protein companies, we are likely to see this trend accelerate.

There is also growing demand for plant-based seafood, and this year's consumption curve looks set to climb well past last year's. The number of plant-based seafood products sold at retail in the US grew by 25% in 2021.

## Beyond the Plate

**Baby food:** As adults' diets change, so do their concerns about what they feed their children. We are seeing a rise in baby-food brands creating "forward-looking" products aimed at building healthy, lifelong eating habits for the next generation. Since these children will grow up seeing plant-based eating as the norm, this is a small preview of future growth across the wider plant-based category.

**Plant-based snacks:** It's worth remembering that many of our favourite snacks are already plant-based. That said, we'll be seeing more of them — made with upcycled plants, regenerative agriculture and other new methods.
